# Known witnesses and limits

The shipped baseline makes no probe and abstains, so its normalized public score is exactly zero.
The evaluator also contains a truth-blind witness that enumerates every graph in the current
four-heavy-atom grammar and spends one charged query on every elementary directed edge. It reaches
one on the frozen worlds, but is deliberately not a scalable solution.

No HY3 or other model calibration has been run for this candidate package. The current tests prove
contract behavior and deterministic headroom only; they do not establish model difficulty,
long-horizon improvement, chemical realism or prospective discovery.
